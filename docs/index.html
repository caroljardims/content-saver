Content Saver

Save what you want to come back to.

A browser extension that keeps articles, selections and links in storage you own — not on somebody else’s server.

There is no server behind this. No account to create, no database holding your reading, no analytics. Everything saves to your own browser first, so it is instant and works offline. Connect your Google Drive and it syncs across browsers through your own account.

What it does

  • Save a whole page The readable article text, without the navigation and ads.
  • Save a selection Highlight a paragraph, right-click, done.
  • Read it back in place Expand any capture in the popup — no round trip to another app.
  • Sync through your own Drive A private folder only this extension can see. Optional.

Privacy

The extension reads a page only when you ask it to save that page, and only the tab you are looking at. It does not track browsing, does not run in the background on sites you visit, and sends nothing to the developer. The source is public, so you do not have to take that on trust.
